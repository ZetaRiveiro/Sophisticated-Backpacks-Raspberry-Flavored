Fork of Sophisticated Backpacks by p3pp3rf1y rebalanced with Raspberry Flavored in mind.\
# Changes\
1. Removed Diamond and Netherite backpacks due to having way to many slots and upgrade slots.\
2. Removed most Upgrades:\
   -Magnet Upgrade: Makes magnet type items in RF redundant\
   -Filter Upgrade: Makes backpacks too reliable as a filtering system for contraptions. In the same way all advanced upgrades have been removed due to similar\
   reasons.\
   -Void Upgrade: Similar reasons to Filter Upgrade.\
   -Compacting Upgrade: Makes create automation obsolete.\
   -Feeding Upgrade: Too brain dead lol.\
   -Smoking / Blasting Upgrades: Removed as those items have been either changed or removed in RF.\
   -Stonecutter Upgrade:Redundant as RF sawing table is better in every single way.\
   -Stack Upgrades & Downgrades: Extremely overpowered making backpacks the single best way to store anything. Also feels way to "mody" to surpass the 64 stack\       limit in a pack that tries to stay vanilla-esc.\
   -Jukebox Upgrade: Redundant with the boombox item.\
   -Inception Upgrade: Makes Terrabins and Sacks completely obsolete.\
   -Everlasting Upgrade: Obsolete due to the "ghosts" that store your items when you die. Also no void due to RF having no End.\
   -Toolswapper Upgrade: Too brain dead lol. Again.\
   -Tank Upgrade: Liquids are hard to work with in RF, making this upgrade, that, is fine in Vanilla Minecraft, extremely op in RF.\
   -Pump Upgrade: Same as above.\
   -Battery Upgrade: Useless due to no LV or HV in RF.\
   -Experience Pump Upgrade: Useless as there is no EXP in RF.\
   -Anvil Upgrade/Smithing Upgrade: This are just personal taste. I feel crafting and smelting are basic enough to be integrated into the backpack, but these two cross the line for me.\
   Upgrades left:
   - Upgrade Base.\
   - Crafting Upgrade.\
   - Smelting Upgrade.\
   - Refill Upgrade.\
   - Deposit Upgrade.\
   - Restock Upgrade.\
   - Pickup Upgrade.\
4. Removed backpacks from generating in chests.\
6. Changed recipes to use RF items.\
